    Default working definitions (edit DIRECTIONAL_RULES in code to refine): sell-side liquidity = resting liquidity below prior lows; buy-side liquidity = resting liquidity above prior highs. A bullish setup is expected to sweep sell-side liquidity before a bullish FVG/OB forms; a bearish setup is expected to sweep buy-side liquidity before a bearish FVG/OB forms. These are provisional and do not block the score — they're flagged for review only.
    How to read this: the confluence score is a count of rules explicitly defined by your framework — it is not a win probability. "SETUP QUALIFIES" requires both 7/7 confluence and R:R at or above your minimum; either one alone is not enough. No claim is made about how often a setup like this actually reaches TP before SL — that comes later, once Phase 2 (journal) and Phase 3 (statistics) have real historical data behind them.
